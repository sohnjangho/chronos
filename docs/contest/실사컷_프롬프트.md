# 실사풍 4컷 「당겨도 소용없던 밤」 — 이미지 생성 프롬프트

> 넣는 곳: 기획서 1-1 〔시각 2〕. 네 컷을 2×2 로 놓고 컷 아래에 캡션을 조판에서 얹는다.
> 표기(필수): 그림 아래 작게 「이해를 돕기 위한 연출 이미지(AI 생성)」.
> 이미지 안에는 글자를 넣지 않는다. 대사·설명은 전부 캡션으로.

## 쓰는 법

1. 아래 **공통 설정**을 네 컷 프롬프트 앞에 매번 붙인다(인물·색감이 컷마다 달라지지 않게).
2. 도구에 같은 인물을 유지하는 기능(참조 이미지, character reference, seed 고정 등)이 있으면 1컷 결과를 2~4컷의 참조로 넣는다.
3. 비율은 **3:2 가로**(한 컷). 2×2 로 놓으면 A4 폭에 맞는다.
4. 얼굴이 크게 나오면 다시 뽑는다. 뒷모습·옆모습·실루엣이 기본이다.
5. 4컷의 모니터 화면은 비워 두고 조판할 때 실제 ChronOS 화면(`assets/rev6/s5-당겨도소용있나.png` 의 카드 부분)을 합성한다.

Same main character in every frame: a Korean construction project manager in his early 40s, short black hair, slim build, wearing a navy work jacket over a light blue collared shirt, a plain white hard hat without any logo, an ID lanyard. Setting: a mid-rise office building construction site in a Korean city (5 stories, reinforced concrete frame, tower crane).

## 컷 1 — 「골조가 크리티컬이다. 당기자.」

```
Evening inside a temporary site office (container office). The project manager stands with his back to the camera in front of a large wall-mounted schedule chart printed on paper — horizontal colored bars in rows, one row highlighted in red, but no readable text. His right hand points at the red bar. On the desk below: rolled drawings, a hard hat, a coffee cup. Through the window behind the chart, the concrete frame of the building and the tower crane in dusk light. Mood: determined, tense.
```
캡션: **"골조가 크리티컬이다. 야간 작업 넣어서 당기자."**

## 컷 2 — 야간 골조

```
Night on the construction site. Wide shot from ground level looking up at the 5th floor of a reinforced concrete frame under bright floodlights. Workers in silhouette placing formwork and rebar on the top slab, a concrete pump boom reaching up, the tower crane lit against a dark blue sky. The project manager stands in the foreground at the lower right, seen from behind, hard hat on, watching. Mood: effort, long night.
```
캡션: **"골조는 예정보다 5일 빨리 끝났다."**

## 컷 3 — 「커튼월이 아직 안 왔다고요?」

```
Overcast morning. The finished 5-story concrete frame stands bare — no facade, no glass, open floors visible, the tower crane idle. In the foreground, the project manager in profile holds a phone to his ear, his free hand on his hip, looking up at the empty building. An empty material laydown area with no curtain wall panels in it. Mood: realization, frustration.
```
캡션: **"커튼월이 아직 안 왔다고요? 공장 제작이 늦어졌다고요?"**

## 컷 4 — 「준공은 하루도 당겨지지 않았다.」

```
Late night back in the site office. Over-the-shoulder shot from behind the project manager sitting at a desk, his head slightly lowered. In front of him a computer monitor facing the camera with a completely blank bright white screen (to be replaced later). On the desk: a printed wall calendar with one date circled in red marker (no readable numbers), a cold coffee cup, his hard hat. A desk lamp gives warm light; the window behind is dark. Mood: quiet, heavy.
```
